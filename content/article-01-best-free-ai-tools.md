## FAQ

### Are free AI tools good enough for college?
Yes. Free tiers of ChatGPT, Notion, and Canva handle most undergraduate work. Pay only when you hit clear limits.

### Is using AI for homework cheating?
Using AI to learn and outline is fine at most schools; submitting AI output as your own work is not. Check your school's policy.

### Which free AI tool is best for math?
ChatGPT and Gemini can walk through step-by-step solutions. Wolfram Alpha (free tier limited) is better for complex calculations.
